# Netheril’s Fall

Step through time into the height of Netheril—an empire of flying cities, epic magic, and looming catastrophe.

* **Time-traveling adventure hooks** that plunge your party into one of the most dangerous eras in Forgotten Realms history.​
* **A gazetteer of High and Low Netheril,** filled with soaring cityscapes, secret underbellies, and factions whose choices shape destiny.​
* **Pivotal historical events** reimagined as playable adventures that let you stand at the edge of the empire’s fall.​
* **8 pre-cataclysmic magic items** to discover and bring back to the present.​
* **8 stat blocks for dangerous creatures,** so you can battle phaerimm and time-lost horrors.
