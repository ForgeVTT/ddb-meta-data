# Project\_Lamordia

> Tomorrow’s horrors are yours to run tonight.

PROJECT\_LAMORDIA reimagines one of Ravenloft’s most chilling Domains of Dread through a modern lens. In the world of Gothic Earth, Dr. Viktra Mordenheim uses cutting-edge technology and forbidden magic to promise digital immortality at the cost of one’s own soul. Plunge your players into a modernistic AI-gone-wrong nightmare in Mordenheim Kinetics facility, a laboratory filled with mechanistic terrors and failed experiments.

Inside this adventure, you will discover:

* 3 one-shots that can be connected into a full adventure, each with a map to traverse through the haunted halls of Mordenheim Kinetics
* 1 character species, the Reborn, whose consciousness was uploaded into a synthetic body after death
* 4 new character backgrounds and 4 new feats (Dark Gifts)
* New mechanics to help you play desperate survivors in a modern world, including 4 preset playable stat blocks
* 5 monstrous stat blocks blending gothic dread and technological horror
