# Valda’s Spire of Secrets: Player Pack 2

The lich Valda has collected a grim menagerie of petrified adventurers within his Spire of Secrets. This Player Pack contains the best of his collection: feats, spells, magic items, familiars, and subclasses! Of course, he’ll probably turn you into stone too if he catches you. Venture into Valda’s Spire of Secrets and expand your D&D horizons, if you dare!
