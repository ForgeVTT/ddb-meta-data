# Heliana’s Guide to Monster Hunting: Part 2

Continue your Heliana's experience with this fifth edition supplement for players who want thrilling hunts, powerful new options, and delicious rewards. This supplement includes five adventures featuring iconic D&D monsters reimagined as deadly multi-wave bosses, supported by a menagerie of 40+ new creatures—each designed to scale across three levels of play and fit seamlessly into your campaign.
