# Shadows of Sithicus

The recent designs of Lord Soth, the Darklord of Sithicus, have destabilized the domain, and the consequences of his actions have created other crises. An unlikely ally has summoned you to deal with an emerging threat that could overwhelm Sithicus and spread beyond its borders to other Domains of Dread.

This adventure series includes:

* A special D&D Encounters Celebration event experience called “Grave Awakenings,” featuring play for one to four groups of adventurers working towards a collective goal, playable in 3 hours.
* 4 D&D Encounters weekly play sessions traveling to the domains of Tepest, Darkon, Borca, and Valachan, each playable in 2 hours or less.
