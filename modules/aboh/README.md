# Astarion's Book of Hungers

Embrace the seductive darkness of Baldur’s Gate with a shadow-kissed expansion full of blood, danger, and intrigue.

This 32-page digital supplement includes:

* **3 vampire-themed backgrounds** to shape your character’s origin in shadow.
* **16 vampiric feats and an updated Dhampir species**, so you can embrace your inner darkness.
* **Play with Astarion in your adventuring group**—two play-ready adventures allow you to team up with your favorite vampire spawn at either character level 3 or character level 12.
* **5 villainous stat blocks**, including infernalist vampires and servants of Mephistopheles, that bring gothic horror into your campaign.
* **Rat’s Run tavern setting**, a fully mapped adventure setting full of secrets, smuggling, and seduction that can be tied into Baldur’s Gate 3 canon.
