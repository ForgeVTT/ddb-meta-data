# Dragon of Icespire Peak

**A Introductionary Adventure for Levels 1-7**

The Beginning adventure to the Essentials Kit Bundle. A D&D adventure is a collection of locations, quests, and challenges that inspires you to tell a story. The outcome of that story is determined by the actions and decisions of the adventurers — and, of course, the luck of the dice. You can run <span>Dragon of Icespire Peak</span>

 for as few as one player or as many as five players. Each player starts with a 1st-level character.<br>
