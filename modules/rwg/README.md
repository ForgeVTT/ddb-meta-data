# Red Wizards’ Gambit

High-ranking agents of Szass Tam, the lich zulkir of Thay, have appeared in the nearby land of Rashemen, a land with a strong tie to spirit magic. Their incursion and magical machinations must be stopped, lest the land of Rashemen be vulnerable to a full-scale attack.

This adventure series includes:

* A special D&D Encounters Celebration event experience called “_Stolen Spirits_,” featuring play for one to four groups of adventurers working towards a collective goal, playable in 3 hours
* 4 D&D Encounters weekly play sessions where you unravel the nefarious plans of the Red Wizards in Rashemen, each playable in 2 hours or less
* 6 quickplay maps for use on Maps VTT
