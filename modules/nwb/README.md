# Northlands Worldbook

Dragonships carve through icy seas, mighty heroes share the hearth with cunning gods, and the song of battle echoes across the land—_welcome to the Northlands._

* **50+ new monsters** to challenge parties at every tier of play.
* **8 new subclasses** and **9 playable species** to forge truly unique heroes.
* **78 new spells** and expanded rune magic to twist fate and unleash powerful effects.
* **9 distinct factions** to ally with or oppose.
* Epic rituals that reshape destiny and fate.
* Downtime systems, a prophecy generator, and robust DM tools.
* **10+ legendary regions** to explore, including the Kingdoms of the Giants.
