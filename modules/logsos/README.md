# Legends of Greyhawk: Saviors of Safeton

Delve into the dangers that plague the beleaguered communities of the Wild Coast and set things right! Three adventures for levels 1-4 and an introductory adventure for levels 1-2 are included in this bundle. Approved for play in the Legends of Greyhawk organized play campaign or for use as compelling one-shots for your home games.
