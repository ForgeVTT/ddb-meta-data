# Journeys through the Radiant Citadel

#### **<span>Light Your Path To Adventure</span>

**

<span><span>Through the mists of the Ethereal Plane shines the Radiant Citadel. Travelers from across the multiverse flock to this mysterious bastion to share their traditions, stories, and calls for heroes. A crossroads of wonders and adventures, the Radiant Citadel is the first step on the path to legend. Where will your journeys take you?</span></span>

<span>Each of the 13 adventures can be run by themselves or dropped in as part of an ongoing campaign. Explore this rich and varied collection of adventures in magical lands including:</span>

- <span><strong>Thirteen&nbsp;NEW&nbsp;stand-alone adventures</strong>&nbsp;spanning levels 1 to 14, each with its own set of maps</span>

- <span>Introduces the&nbsp;<strong>Radiant Citadel</strong>, a new location on the Ethereal Plane that connects adventurers to richly detailed and distinct corners of the D&amp;D multiverse</span>

- <span>Each adventure can be dropped into&nbsp;<strong>any existing Dungeon &amp; Dragons campaign</strong>&nbsp;setting or&nbsp;<strong>on worlds of your own design</strong></span>

- <span>Introduces&nbsp;<strong>eleven&nbsp;NEW&nbsp;D&amp;D monsters</strong>&nbsp;for players to discover</span>

- <span>There’s a story for every adventuring party, from&nbsp;<strong>whimsical</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>light</strong>&nbsp;to&nbsp;<strong>dark</strong>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<strong>foreboding</strong>&nbsp;and everything in between</span>
