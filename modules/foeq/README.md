# Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone

> Discover Eberron’s wild Western Frontier with setting creator Keith Baker!

Journey across the Western Frontier, the contested land that lies between the kingdom of Breland and the monster nation of Droaam. _Frontiers of Eberron: Quickstone_ takes a deep dive into this untamed region, exploring the towns and denizens of this dangerous land. Discover new character options and play as a harpy, gargoyle or medusa species in the introductory “Heart of Stone” adventure, all included in this adventure sourcebook!
