# The Gunslinger Class: Valda’s Spire of Secrets

Gunslingers live on the edge, brandishing six-shooters and shotguns to survive by the skin of their teeth. You’re a maverick, cool under pressure and deadly in a quick-draw. Gunslingers may have few friends, but your enemies will be quaking in their boots.

Play the Gunslinger class if you enjoy scoring powerful Critical Hits and having an entire arsenal of guns at your disposal. Take aim with 6 subclasses and a host of new maneuvers powered by Risk Dice.
