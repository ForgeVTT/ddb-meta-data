# One-Shot Wonders: Holiday Adventure Pack

> ‘Tis the season to roll initiative!

Bring joy and chaos to your table this Winterfest season, with sleigh chases, pudding gremlin fights and toy factory break-ins. This festive collection is bursting with ready-to-play one-shots, battle maps, playable species, magic items, and more for merry misadventures. Each one-shot is perfect for dropping into your campaign, or running as a special holiday game night!

What’s inside:

* Five holiday adventures (for levels 1–12), each playable in three hours
* Five detailed maps, including a toy factory, ice castle, banquet hall and more
* Three festive-themed monsters ready to battle your party
* Four wintry playable species, from the Snowborn to the reindeer-like Tarandus
* Twelve magic items based on the Twelve Days of Christmas
